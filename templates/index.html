ECONOVA

Learn economics through the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

ECONOVA is an economics learning platform for high school students. You connect ideas like markets, growth, inequality, finance and trade to real-world problems, and you study on your own, at your own pace.

Get started (Sign up) Log in

What you can do on ECONOVA

  • 9 economics courses — Each course has 3 lessons. Pick a level (beginner, intermediate or advanced), read, then check your understanding with lesson quizzes and a final assessment.
  • Diagnostic assessment — Get a recommended starting level (just a suggestion; you can change it anytime).
  • Economics Lab — Change the values in supply-and-demand and carbon tax models and calculate the results yourself.
  • AI Tutor and Debate — Ask questions based on approved learning materials and think about issues from several perspectives. AI never decides your score.
  • Learning report and SDG Capstone project — Look back on your progress and write your own solution to an SDG problem.
