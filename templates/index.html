How you learn

Four steps, in this order. Stop and come back at any point; your progress is saved to your account.

  1. 1

    Take the diagnostic

    Short questions on the ideas behind all nine courses show which areas you already know and suggest a level.

  2. 2

    Learn at your level

    Every lesson has a beginner, intermediate and advanced version. Switch at any time.

  3. 3

    Practise and check

    Quizzes are graded by fixed rules on the server. Worked examples and simulations show the reasoning.

  4. 4

    Apply it to the SDGs

    The capstone asks you to analyse a development problem and propose a policy.

Example conversation

If ice cream gets cheaper, is that a change in demand?

Not by itself. A lower price moves you along the same demand curve, so it changes the quantity demanded. Demand changes when something other than the good's own price shifts, such as income or tastes.From the lesson "Supply and Demand"
