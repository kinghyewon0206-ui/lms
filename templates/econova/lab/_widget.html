Model assumptions and how to read the results
  • The model assumes straight-line demand and supply, perfect competition, and a per-unit tax charged to producers. The split of the burden is the same no matter who legally pays the tax.
  • The "deadweight loss" is the gain from trade the market loses if we assume there is no external damage such as pollution.
  • If you enter an external damage (md), the model also counts the damage avoided because less is traded. Total social welfare can then rise or fall. A carbon tax does not always reduce welfare.
  • md and e are assumed values for illustration. They are not the real social cost of carbon or real emissions factors.
