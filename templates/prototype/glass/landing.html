How you learn

Four steps, in this order. You can stop and come back at any point; your progress is saved to your account.

ECONOVA Tutor · Markets & Prices, Lesson 1

If ice cream gets cheaper, is that a change in demand?

Not by itself. A lower price moves you along the same demand curve, so it changes the quantity demanded. Demand changes when something other than the good's own price shifts, such as income or tastes.From this lesson: "Demand vs. quantity demanded"
